## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, durable, and highly functional digital environment for commercial building materials and construction logistics. It bridges field operations with institutional enterprise procurement, catering to contractors, quantity surveyors, project managers, and independent builders.

The visual aesthetic synthesizes **Modern Industrial Utilitarianism** with **Architectural Precision**:
- **Durable Authority:** Grounded in deep slate and iron armatures, projecting supply stability, operational compliance, and structural integrity.
- **High-Visibility Functional Accents:** Alert and call-to-action touchpoints leverage high-vis safety orange and industrial amber to guide action decisively under high-glare or mobile on-site conditions.
- **Spec-Driven Hierarchy:** Information architecture prioritizes technical legibility—yield ratios, dimensional specifications, minimum order quantities (MOQ), and certifications (e.g., SNI/ISO standards)—without visual clutter.
- **Atmosphere:** Rigorous, unpretentious, dependable, and swift. Elements evoke structural blueprints and heavy logistics rather than consumer lifestyle whimsy.

## Colors

The palette leverages industrial field codes: high-durability steel, raw architectural aggregates, and high-visibility safety markers.

- **Primary Action (`#EA580C` - Safety Orange):** Reserved strictly for primary commercial actions (ordering, price calculation, bulk request, and checkout). It commands immediate focus against slate surfaces.
- **Secondary Base (`#0F172A` - Heavy Industrial Slate):** Forms the structural spine of the system—navigation frames, high-level headers, active state fills, and foundational structural boundaries. A secondary tint (`#1E293B`) supports interior container panels.
- **Tertiary Validation (`#10B981` - Verified Emerald):** Utilized exclusively for supply assurance signals: "Ready Stock", "SNI Certified", "TKDN Compliant", and confirmed logistics status.
- **Neutral Backbone:**
  - Base Page Canvas: `#F8FAFC` (Concrete Off-White) creates a glare-free, pristine backdrop.
  - Surface Containers: `#FFFFFF` for product grids, data sheets, and invoice cards.
  - Structural Borders: `#E2E8F0` for low-noise partition lines.
  - Subdued Elements: `#64748B` for auxiliary technical parameters and metadata.
- **Alert / Warning Accent (`#F59E0B` - Construction Amber):** Applied to inventory thresholds, lead-time warnings, bulk tier break markers, and pending status tags.

## Typography

The typography uses a single geometric sans-serif family across all roles to maximize technical clarity, eliminate unnecessary asset overhead, and maintain visual consistency across dense spreadsheets and material specification tables.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides wide proportions, distinct numeral glyphs, and high x-height, critical for reading complex material specs (e.g., bar diameters, cement compressive strength, dimensional tolerances).
- **Tabular & Technical Numbers:** Material codes, unit rates, weights, and batch volumes must always render with proportional tabular alignments to prevent layout shift during recalculations.
- **Spec-Numerical Tier:** Dedicated for price tags, dimension markers, and calculator output displays, utilizing strong 700 and 800 weights to ensure zero ambiguity in the field under direct sunlight.

## Layout & Spacing

The system is constructed on a 12-column architectural grid for desktop environments, reflowing to a 4-column structured grid for mobile on-site operations.

- **Desktop (min-width: 1280px):** 12 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Maximum content container is capped at 1440px to prevent excessive line length in technical specs.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Sidebar panels collapse into modal drawers.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 0.75rem`. Sticky operational bars for quick checkout and material recalculation persist at the bottom viewport.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an unambiguous 4px / 8px incremental scale. Tightly clustered pairings (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) bind measurement inputs and unit labels, while distinct section boundaries rely on `space-lg` and `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

To reflect solid construction fundamentals, this design system minimizes soft gradients and floating blurs in favor of **Structural Outlines and Controlled Depth**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Concrete off-white (`#F8FAFC`). Flat, non-reflective base plane.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a structural 1px outline (`#E2E8F0`). Flat elevation by default; no shadow.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Hover & Focused Cards):** Elevates via a sharp industrial shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, paired with a border shift to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals, Technical Calculators, Flyouts):** Solid elevated depth: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, framed with a solid 1px border (`#0F172A`).
- **Surface Inset:** Material specification tables and formula input wells use an inset background (`#F1F5F9`) with a recessed border (`#E2E8F0`) to feel structurally embedded into the interface.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft Structural Shapes (`roundedness: 1`)**. 

- **Containers & Technical Cards:** Use `4px` (`0.25rem`) corner radiuses, reflecting machine-cut materials, steel framing, and concrete modular blocks. Excessive roundness is strictly avoided to preserve technical rigor.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons):** Configured with uniform `4px` corners for clean alignment against adjacent grid cells.
- **Badges and Status Tags:** Scaled to `2px` or full pill execution only when designating regulatory certifications (such as SNI or TKDN) to distinguish metadata from operational buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Commercial CTA):** Safety Orange (`#EA580C`) background, white text, bold weight (`600`), 4px radius. On hover: `#C2410C`. Used for "Beli Sekarang", "Minta Penawaran", "Hitung Kebutuhan".
- **Secondary Action (Inquiry/Save):** Industrial Slate (`#0F172A`) solid fill, white text. On hover: `#1E293B`.
- **Structural Outline Button:** 1.5px border (`#0F172A`), transparent background, `#0F172A` text.
- **Destructive/Cancel:** White background, 1px border (`#FCA5A5`), `#DC2626` text.

### Material Cards
- Pure white container, 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), 4px corner radius.
- Header contains clear product title, SKU code, and manufacturer stamp.
- Features high-contrast pricing with associated structural measurement units locked inline (e.g., `Rp 68.500` `/sak`, `Rp 142.000` `/m²`, `Rp 84.000` `/batang`).
- Includes a dedicated row for technical badges above the fold.

### Technical Badges & Certification Pills
- Small-scale, high-density tags (`label-sm`), uppercase with bold weighting.
- **SNI / Quality Certified:** Solid Emerald tint (`#ECFDF5`), Emerald text (`#047857`), 1px border (`#A7F3D0`).
- **Ready Stock:** Slate-tinted base (`#F1F5F9`), Dark Slate text (`#0F172A`), border (`#CBD5E1`).
- **Grosir / Bulk Tier:** Amber tint (`#FFFBEB`), Dark Amber text (`#B45309`), border (`#FDE68A`).

### Material Volume Calculator
- Inset container (`#F1F5F9`) structured with strict numerical inputs.
- Segmented unit switches (e.g., "m³", "m²", "Batang", "Truk").
- Interactive steppers accompanied by live estimated tonnage, delivery truck volume capacity, and total dynamic pricing.

### Inputs & Quantity Selectors
- Height: 44px (touch-compliant for job site field tablets).
- 1px border (`#CBD5E1`), background `#FFFFFF`. Active focus border shifts to `#0F172A` with a 2px outer outline at 15% opacity.
- Integrated measurement postfix badges (e.g., `kg`, `zak`, `ton`, `meter`) locked inside the right boundary with `#64748B` typography.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Radios
- Square 18px checkboxes with 2px corner radius. Border `#94A3B8`, active fill `#EA580C` with a bold white check mark.
- Bulk selection grids leverage table-row toggles with prominent selected highlights (`#FFF7ED`).
